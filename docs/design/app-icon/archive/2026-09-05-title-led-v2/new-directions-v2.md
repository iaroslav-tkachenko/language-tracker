# Title-led concept studies — v2

- Date: 2026-09-05
- Status: Four v2 studies generated; awaiting product-owner review
- Tool: Built-in OpenAI image generation
- Reference role: The supplied Preply and Memrise screenshots were used only for
  broad composition, hierarchy, and flat visual confidence. Their proprietary
  marks, letterforms, and exact palettes were explicitly excluded.
- Global constraint: No heatmap imagery.

## 1. Speech Clock + Title

File: `concept-01-speech-clock-title-v2.png`

Prompt request: create an original Language Tracker icon with a minimal black
line symbol in the upper half and the exact title `Language Tracker` centered
below on two lines. Combine an open speech path and a small circular time marker
into one continuous emblem. Use a full-bleed coral-pink `#F875A6` background and
near-black `#111318` artwork. Avoid flags, books, globes, translator symbols,
effects, mockups, and resemblance to the supplied brands.

Assessment: clear language-plus-time meaning and strong title hierarchy. The
symbol is still too literal and should become more distinctive if selected.

## 2. Dialogue L + Title

File: `concept-02-dialogue-l-title-v2.png`

Prompt request: create an original white emblem above the exact two-line title
`Language Tracker`. Form the emblem from two rounded speech strokes interlocking
into an abstract forward-moving L, with one emerald tracked-moment dot. Use a
full-bleed primary-blue `#155DFC` background, off-white `#F8FAFC`, and emerald
`#34D399`. Exclude flags, heatmaps, power/play symbols, effects, mockups, and
resemblance to known language-learning logos.

Assessment: strongest connection to the existing product palette and the most
promising symbol-over-title composition. The emblem needs manual simplification
and more intentional negative space.

## 3. Country Row + Title

File: `concept-03-country-row-title-v2.png`

Prompt request: set the exact words `LANGUAGE` and `TRACKER` as two large stacked
lines. Along the bottom, place circular medallions for Spain, France, Germany,
and an outlined plus. Use a full-bleed golden-yellow `#F7C95E` background and
deep navy `#172033` typography with restrained flag colors. Exclude heatmaps,
mascots, effects, mockups, and the supplied brands' proprietary typography.

Assessment: the most immediately understandable multi-language direction and
closest to the requested Memrise-like hierarchy. The flags may imply a fixed
catalog or nation-language equivalence, and their detail will not survive small
PWA sizes without simplification.

## 4. Language Glyph Row + Title

File: `concept-04-language-row-title-v2.png`

Prompt request: set the exact title `LANGUAGE TRACKER` in two bold stacked lines.
Below it, place speech medallions containing `Ñ`, `あ`, and `A`, followed by an
outlined plus. Use a full-bleed deep emerald `#0F8A6C` background, off-white
title, and yellow, blue, and coral accents. Exclude heatmaps, flags, globes,
effects, mockups, and resemblance to the supplied brands.

Assessment: communicates multilingual scope without tying a language to a
country. It is more inclusive than flags, though the small glyphs require a
larger-than-usual icon treatment and may disappear at 32 px.

## Decision gate

No visual is selected. The next iteration should develop one chosen composition
into two or three controlled variants. Production SVG construction and export
remain blocked until a specific visual variant is explicitly approved.
