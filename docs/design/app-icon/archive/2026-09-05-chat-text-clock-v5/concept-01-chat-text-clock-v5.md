## Prompt

Create a refined original upper icon close in structure to the owner-supplied
reference: one horizontally oriented rounded dialogue-window outline with a
short lower-left tail and a small circular clock overlapping the window at its
lower-right corner. Add exactly two short parallel horizontal lines inside the
window to symbolize text, keep them clear of the clock, and place only two
simple hands inside the clock. Preserve the exact two-line title
`Language Tracker` below. Use a full-bleed primary-blue `#155DFC` background,
off-white `#F8FAFC` linework and title, and a restrained emerald `#34D399` clock
face. Keep the mark flat, monoline, rounded, vector-friendly, and free of
heatmaps, flags, extra text, gradients, effects, mockups, and known-brand
imitation.

## Assessment

The result follows the requested hierarchy and symbol structure: the dialogue
window is the primary mark, two internal strokes communicate text, and the
smaller clock overlaps the lower-right corner without becoming the main icon.
The geometry is suitable for clean manual SVG reconstruction after approval,
but stroke widths, exact overlap, and title spacing should not be treated as
production coordinates yet.
